# Mars retrograde loop

You are standing on [Earth](objekt:earth) and looking at [Mars](objekt:mars). Earth runs around
the [Sun](objekt:sun) faster than Mars does, because it is closer to it. It overtakes Mars, just
like a car on the motorway overtakes a slower one. During the overtaking, seen from Earth, Mars
looks as if it were running backwards for a few weeks; in reality it never does. In this scene time
runs fast, and a line shows the path of Mars across the sky.
