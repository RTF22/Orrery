# From Neptune to the distant Sun

The camera stands behind [Neptune](objekt:neptune), the outermost planet, and looks at it. Only
a narrow side is lit by the distant [Sun](objekt:sun), which stands next to it in the frame; the
simulation still brightens the dark side a little. From here the Sun is thirty times further
away than from the [Earth](objekt:earth). In reality it would look tiny, like a very bright
star, and it is bitterly cold. Its light travels for just over four hours to get here.
